Support Desk FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What physical qualifications are required for Level 5 climbs? +

Level 5 climbing tours are technically demanding and require prior high-altitude cardios, crampon navigation experience, and safety permits log verification through our climber board dashboard.

Are satellite tracking devices provided during climbs? +

Yes. All tours include active satellite tracking telemetry nodes which log climber coordinates directly to base centers and emergency med-evac responders.

What is your expedition cancellation and booking policy? +

Tours cancelled 30 days prior to departure are eligible for a full booking refund. Check our Cancellation Policies section or speak directly with our guide coordinators.
